Things I've built.

Posture Coach

Android · On-device AI

An Android app that works as a personal gym coach. It analyzes your exercise form in real time through the phone camera and gives instant spoken feedback, with no trainer needed.

It uses a dual-layer AI design. ML Kit Pose Detection runs entirely on the device at 60fps, tracking 33 body landmarks and calculating joint angles for instant form corrections, with zero latency and zero API calls. A Gemini Vision model steps in only at biomechanically critical moments, such as the bottom of a squat or the lowest point of a push-up, and delivers natural-language coaching through text-to-speech.

It supports six exercises (squats, push-ups, lunges, plank, overhead press and deadlift) with automatic rep counting, a voice assistant for questions mid-workout, and smart API throttling that keeps usage within free-tier limits.

What makes it novel. Joint-angle thresholds act as biomechanical event detectors that decide when to call the vision model. That cuts redundant API calls while keeping the analysis sharpest at the moments that matter. This approach is being explored for a provisional patent filing.

Kotlin · Jetpack Compose · CameraX · ML Kit · Gemini API · Retrofit · Android TTS

Pitches and hackathons.

WePitch 2.0

1st place · Team Phoenix · IEDC Bootcamp CEC

Our first pitching competition, and we almost walked out after the first few pitches. We stayed, pitched ThreadConnect, and took first place.

ThreadConnect is a platform that connects customers with skilled women tailors, focused on consistent quality, faster deliveries and real visibility for a workforce that is largely missing from the digital economy. A large share of India's tailoring workforce is women, yet most have no digital presence, no steady income pipeline and no way to grow beyond their immediate circle.

Instead of jumping to solutions, we studied how tailors actually work today, where the friction is, and what a platform-driven model could realistically look like. Built with two teammates.

NASA Space Apps

Team AISA MACE · Low Earth Orbit

Our entry in the world's largest hackathon, from a mixed team of an IPM and CSE s. Our problem statement was the commercialisation of Low Earth Orbit.

Our solution makes space tourism a reality, starting with high-altitude balloon capsules and expanding into suborbital and orbital tourism. The goal is luxurious, safe and sustainable experiences for global travellers, operating at altitudes of 20 to 25 miles and offering a 360° view of Earth without extreme G-forces. We also mapped the operational challenges and how to mitigate them.
